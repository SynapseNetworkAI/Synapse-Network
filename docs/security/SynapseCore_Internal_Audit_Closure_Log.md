# SynapseCore Internal Audit Closure Log

Closure log means the evidence table that proves every audit finding has a final
state. Each row records what was found, why it matters, how it was fixed or
accepted, which command proves it, who closed it, and when.

Critical and High findings must be `Closed`; they cannot be closed by risk
acceptance alone.
